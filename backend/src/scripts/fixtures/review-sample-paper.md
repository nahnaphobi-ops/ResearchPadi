# Mobile Learning and Study Habits among Nursing Students in a Ghanaian College

**Abstract**

This study looks at how nursing students use mobile phones for study. A survey of 120 students was carried out. Most students used their phones to read lecture notes. The results was significant for students who studied in groups.

## CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION

Mobile phones are now common among students in Ghana (Asante, 2021). Many students use them to read notes, watch videos and talk with classmates. Mobile learning improves exam results for every student in every college. This study asks how nursing students at one college use their phones for study.

The purpose of this study is to describe these study habits. The purpose of this study is to describe these study habits so that tutors can plan better.

## CHAPTER 2: LITERATURE REVIEW

Earlier work found that students who read notes on their phones spent more time studying (Boateng & Owusu, 2019). Other studies reported that group chats helped students share past questions (Asante, 2021). A large review showed that phone use in class always lowers attention (Kwarteng, 2018).

## CHAPTER 3: METHODOLOGY

A cross-sectional survey was used. The sample was 120 nursing students chosen by convenience sampling. Data were collected with a short questionnaire and analysed with descriptive statistics.

## CHAPTER 4: RESULTS

Of the 120 students, 98 said they read lecture notes on their phones every week. Students who studied in groups reported more study hours than those who studied alone.

## CHAPTER 5: DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION

The findings agree with Boateng and Owusu (2019). In summary, mobile phones play a role in how these students study. Tutors may use group chats to share materials.
